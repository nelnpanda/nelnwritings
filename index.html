    The Grip

    August 7Poetry

    I held the pain like a keepsake,
    not because I loved it,
    but because I feared forgetting
    what it meant to feel.

    It was my proof—
    that I had lived,
    that I had loved,
    that something once mattered enough to break me.

    So I gripped it,
    tight enough to bleed,
    tight enough to carve its name into my bones
    so I wouldn’t lose the memory
    of what it meant to be whole.

    But pain isn’t the only proof.
    There’s strength in softness,
    truth in healing,
    and feeling doesn’t fade when the wound does.

    I can let go now—
    not because I’m forgetting,
    but because I’m remembering
    how to feel without bleeding.

    The Shadowy Maze

    Poetry

    Corrupted and tortured soul,
    Tormented by past suppressed trauma,
    Left broken and alone, filled with rage,
    Fighting the mental anguish,
    Losing against the feeling in the brain.
    Battling urges within distorted perception,
    Voices and demons surround like the blackest of night,
    With no hope in sight, with no reason to cry, I slowly die.
    I mourn the death of my happiness and sense of direction.
    Alone and lost, I stand with no purpose.
    I wander, watching many dark moons pass,
    Unable to find an escape from the shadowy maze.
    I find myself turned around and confused,
    With no end in sight, nothing feels right.

    Letters for Strangers

    Letter to the Stranger Who Needs to Hear This

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    You are beautiful in the highest of highs and the lowest of lows.

    And when it feels like everything is for naught, listen closely for the quiet, harmonious tones of love and support around you. They may be faint, but if you follow them, they will guide you back to where you need to be.

    Explore the world like sailors of old. Sail the seven seas, wander far, see the wonders of the world, and pause to appreciate life's simple beauties. It is often in the smallest moments that inner peace is found.

    May you find your way again.

    — A stranger rooting for you

    Letter for When Our Paths Cross

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    If our paths ever cross and we find ourselves speaking of life and its intricacies, know that no matter how hard we try, the limits of our own perspective make it impossible to fully understand one another's stories and feelings.

    Each of us walks a road shaped by experiences no one else has lived, and so we can only try to relate through what we ourselves have known.

    I may never understand your pain or your joy exactly as you feel it, but I will try to listen, to relate where I can, and to offer support where it is needed.

    Sometimes, trying is the closest understanding we can give — and sometimes, that is enough.

    — A stranger willing to listen

    Letter to the Stranger Who Feels Alone

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    In a world of billions, the feeling of being alone is all too common. Yet the odds of truly being alone are slim, because somewhere, someone is willing to ask, to listen, to care.

    I hope to be one of those people — the one who reaches out, who brings others together, who reminds you that someone notices you're here.

    We may be strangers, but that does not mean we are alone. Sometimes all it takes is one person to ask, "Are you okay?" to remind us that we belong somewhere.

    So if you ever feel unseen, remember this:

    You are not alone.

    We are strangers, yes — but for this moment, we are together.

    — A stranger who cares

    Letter on Care Without the Pretense of Love

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    I must admit, I do not love you — but I do care, as deeply as the tides roll upon distant shores.

    Love is often spoken so easily, so casually, that its meaning is sometimes lost. I will not pretend to feel what I do not, nor will I speak words simply because they are expected.

    Instead, I will remain true to myself, just as you should remain true to who you are.

    We need not claim love where it does not yet exist. Sometimes, honest care is more genuine than empty words.

    And honesty, in the end, is its own quiet form of kindness.

    — A stranger choosing truth

    Letter for the Stranger in Heartbreak

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    In moments of heartbreak or mourning the loss of another, it can feel as though something within us has shattered beyond repair.

    Yet within those broken pieces lies the opportunity to be born anew. What remains after loss is not only pain, but resilience — and resilience is a quiet beauty every one of us can possess.

    Though loss changes us, it also teaches us how deeply we are capable of caring, and from that, we rise again — not as we were, but as someone stronger, shaped by what we have endured.

    Even in heartbreak, life begins again.

    — A stranger who believes in your strength

    Letter on Belonging

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    The absence of belonging is rarely loud. It is not the sudden loss of one great feeling, but the slow fading of many small ones.

    Belonging is not a single moment or place. It is found in quiet conversations, shared laughter, familiar faces, small kindnesses — the little things that gather over time until we feel we are part of something.

    And when those small moments disappear, the larger sense of belonging fades with them.

    So if you feel you do not belong, know this: sometimes all it takes is one small connection to begin building that feeling again.

    Belonging is not one grand thing.

    It is many small things, added together.

    — A stranger among many

    Letter on Perspective

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    Everything is shaped by opinion — even this very letter before you.

    Each word has been chosen with care, every phrase shaped with thought, from its tone to the feeling it tries to leave behind. Nothing written, spoken, or understood exists without perspective guiding it.

    And so it is with the world itself. Life is not handed to us ready-made. It becomes what we shape it into through our choices, our interpretations, and the meaning we give to our experiences.

    Nothing arrives finished.

    Meaning is something we create.

    — A stranger shaping meaning, too

    Letter on the Search for Peace

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    Peace is not permanent — it is something everyone keeps searching for. For some, it is their reason to keep going. For others, it is something they manage to find each day.

    But there are many who have not yet found their peace and feel ready to give up the search.

    Yet within that searching lie the greatest possibilities — connections with others who have found their peace, with those still searching, or simply discovering something that brings joy.

    And sometimes, within that joy, peace is found, even if only for a moment.

    So keep searching a little longer. You may find more than peace along the way.

    — A stranger still searching, too

    Letter for the Stranger Struggling to Get Out of Bed

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    When it's early in the morning and you struggle to get out of bed, whatever the reason may be, I can only relate to how heavy those moments can feel.

    But every second you remain there is another possibility slowly fading away — another chance for something unexpected, meaningful, or beautiful to happen without you there to see it.

    I believe you will achieve great things. And even if those things do not seem great to you, they will still matter. They will still be victories worth having.

    And somewhere, someone will see them as great — even if that someone is only a stranger like me.

    So rise when you can. Take the day slowly if you must. Just don't give up on it before it begins.

    — A stranger who believes in you

    Letter on Pride

    Letters for Strangers

    Stranger,

    Pride is not something given; it is something taken.

    So take pride in something, whether big or small, for anything you are proud of holds value simply because it matters to you.

    It is not foolish to take pride in what brings you joy — whether it is something as simple as a small rock collection, an ever-growing library of knowledge, or simply the fact that you have made it this far in this strange and complicated thing we call life.

    No achievement is too small if it helped carry you forward.

    Be proud of the things that are yours.

    — A stranger who hopes you see your worth
